What is Health-Bot

The healthcare industry is continually striving to enhance patient experience and treatment outcomes. One effective approach to achieving this is by utilizing artificial intelligence (AI) to deliver personalized predictions and recommendations based on patients’ symptoms and medical records. This project proposes the development of an AI-driven hospital recommendation and disease prediction chatbot that can identify symptoms and suggest suitable hospitals according to the user’s specific medical requirements.
